      The two of us continued to drink and make conversation. It was something that never got old with Ronnie. Even though we've been friends since we were practically in diapers, there's always still things to talk about with one another. She's been my number 1 since the day that we met, and I don't know how I would've made it through my childhood without her by my side. 
      


      We shifted the conversation from me onto her, talking about her current boyfriend Elijah, her upcoming family vacation, and about her job. Unlike me who chose a job that is going to take me over a decade or more of education until I finally get to really start my career, she opted to go to graduate school and become a play therapist for children who suffered abuse. It's really admirable work, and I am so proud of her. 
      


      Two new drinks appeared in front of us, courtesy of the group of guys at the other end of the bar, the bartender let us know. Veronica chuckled and slid one over to me, taking a sip of her own. Two of the guys were looking at us with lust in their eyes, assumingly the ones who bought us drinks, but Veronica mouthed to them "We're taken" 
      


      I watched as their egos deflated and turn away the second Veronica continued to sip her new fruity little drink with a smirk on her face. 
      


      She took another look around the room, almost like she was looking for someone, and slumped her shoulders when she turned back to me. "Slim pickings tonight" 
      


      "For the last time, I'm not going to find the love of my life in a dingy old bar" I playfully scoffed at her. She's been with her boyfriend for about 5 years now, and quite honestly they are the only reason I think the slightest bit of love exists in this world. Every other relationship I've witnessed has been a disaster and it's made me believe that the fairytale doesn't exist. Not to mention the fact that in 26 years of life, I have never found anyone that I've connected with on that level. Of course, I am glad my best friend has her person, but with my history, it doesn't seem to be in the cards no matter how many nights she tries to find me a man. 
      


      "Hey I met Elijah at a concert because I passed out and the hot security guard had to come to my rescue. Love can turn up in unexpected places" She remarked. "And then I threw up on his shoes" 
      


      "Still struggling to understand how he went out with you after that" I teased. I remember hearing this story when it first happened. We were supposed to go to the concert together, but I had to bail last minute for a family emergency. I felt extremely bad, but Veronica is quite independent and didn't mind going by herself. At the end of the day though, I guess it worked out for her?
      


      "I'm not too sure either" She shrugged. "But my point still stands" 
      


      "Whatever you say" I chuckled. 
      


      I looked at the time on my phone, it was about 11:30 and I could feel the effects of the alcohol I've been drinking. I told Veronica I would be heading to the bathroom, and tripped over my own feet a little as I headed there. I opened the door to the bathroom, seeing it was empty and made my way into one of the stalls. I did my business, washed my hands, and fixed my hair in the mirror a little bit. 
      


      When I was done, I headed back over to Veronica who had a mischievous smile on her face. 
      


      "That can't be good" I commented. 
      


      "Be subtle, but look to your right" She whispered, clapping her hands excitedly in front of her. 
      


      My head immediately snapped to my right. 
      


      "Mallory!" She "whisper" scolded. I could tell she was increasingly getting more drunk by the way her whisper was barely able to be called one. 
      


      My eyes fixated on who I knew she was referring to. He must've walked in while I was in the bathroom, because he was not there before. His hair was short, and housed a hair clip on the top of his head, but his eyes were covered by sunglasses. Weird, because it was night time, but as I was looking at him he removed them and shoved them in his pocket. The pocket of his navy loose fitting pants, as well as a white t-shirt and a navy jacket with some words on the back I was too out of it to read right now. When getting a look at his face, I assumed he was a good few years older than I was, but probably not by much. I wasn't going to admit it out loud to her, but out of all the guys she's tried to handpick for me, this is the top contender in the looks category. 
      


      
                                      
                
      
                    
      "You think he's hot" Veronica commented when I turned to her again. "Yes!" 
      


      "Shhh" I giggled, pressing a finger to my lips in a shushing motion. The next thing I said, the alcohol must've spoke for me. "Okay he's a little hot"
      


      "You should go talk to him!" She urged. This is a normal thing for us. We go out, she tries to find a guy for me, I never give in, and the cycle repeats. Some people would find it annoying of her, but the two of us have been friends for so long and we're typically good about really knowing when it's time to quit. I don't really mind it, and sometimes it's just entertainment for us. 
      


      "Nope" I shook my head. I glanced over at him once again, a glass of whiskey now grasped between his ring cladded fingers. Honestly, we weren't too far away from him that I'd be surprised if he wasn't hearing this conversation between us. 
      


      "Oh come on" Veronica sighed dramatically. 
      


      "I'm not going to leave you alone" I shrugged, using it as an excuse and smiling at her. 
      


      "You'd be moving like" She looked, counted on her fingers, "3 chairs" 
      


      "3 too many" I declined. "Shouldn't we go home soon?" 
      


      "Why?" She made a face, ordered another round of drinks from the bartender. 
      


      She was ahead of me by like two drinks at this point, so I was slightly more levelheaded than she was. "Maybe we should call Eli" 
      


      Elijah is our designated pick up driver. Although, it's not that often that he's having to do it. I actually tend to stay away from alcohol for the most part, but tonight I just needed something to take the edge off before I start my residency. 
      


      She debated going back and forth with me before huffing and pulling out her phone. "Fineee" 
      


      I watched as she typed on her phone, and from where I was sitting I could tell it was just a random letters and not a complete sentence. He responded back immediately that he was coming to get us, which made me chuckle. 
      


      "You should go talk to him before we leave" Veronica tried one last time. 
      


      "He looks like he wants to be alone" I inferred, seeing the stressed look on his face. 
      


      "I think he wants company" 
      


      "You want him to have company" I corrected. 
      


      "What if you're missing out on the greatest love because you're too afraid to talk to him?" 
      


      "I'm not afraid" I countered. 
      


      "Then go do it" She smirked. 
      


      "You're crazy" 
      


      "Am not"
      


      "Am too"
      


      "Am not" 
      


      "Am too" 
      


      "Mallory" 
      


      "Veronica" I stared at her. "Okay okay fine" 
      


      Wow. That most recent drink must've really gotten to me. 
      


      "Yes!" She shrieked. 
      


      Some newfound confidence had me checking I looked good in my camera app and sauntering over to this mystery's man direction. Honestly, I was at a loss about what I was supposed to say to him, I don't even know the last time I spoke to a guy. What am I supposed to do? 
      


      The actual answer is probably not what I actually said. When I got closer to him, I could really tell the beautiful features of his face. He looked at me when my presence was known to him, letting me stare into green eyes of his.
